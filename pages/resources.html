Resources & Exercises

Therapist-backed tools to help you breathe, reflect, and heal.

Box Breathing

Used by military and therapists to calm the nervous system fast.

Inhale
4
4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s. Activates your parasympathetic system. Ideal before sleep.

Deep Belly Breathing

Place one hand on your chest, one on your belly. Let your belly expand first. 5 slow breaths.

Alternate Nostril

A yoga technique that balances both hemispheres of the brain. Great for focus and calm.

Humming Breath

Inhale deeply, exhale slowly while humming "mmm." Vibration calms the vagus nerve.

5-4-3-2-1 Technique

Bring yourself back to the present moment using your 5 senses.

👁 SEE

Name 5 things you can see right now.

✋ TOUCH

Notice 4 things you can physically feel.

👂 HEAR

Listen for 3 distinct sounds around you.

👃 SMELL

Identify 2 things you can smell (or remember smells you love).

👅 TASTE

Notice 1 thing you can taste right now.

Cold Water Grounding

Splash cold water on your face or hold an ice cube. The cold activates your diving reflex, slowing heart rate and inducing calm.

Body Scan

Starting from your toes, slowly bring attention to each part of your body. Notice sensations without judgment. Takes 5-10 minutes.

Safe Place Visualization

Close your eyes. Imagine a place where you feel completely safe and calm. Describe it in detail — colors, sounds, temperature, smells. Stay there for 3 minutes.
